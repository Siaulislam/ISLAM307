- Free and potentially commercially distributed mobile/desktop application.
- Permanent offline SQLite or packaged media.

- User-facing excerpts and complete records.
- Optional update downloads.

Private correspondence must not be committed unless publication is authorized.
Store a sanitized decision record, effective dates, approved files, checksums,
attribution text, and restrictions in `LICENSES/<dataset>.md`.
