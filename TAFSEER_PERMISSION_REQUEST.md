# Offline Tafseer permission contacts

No additional Tafseer text may be imported until written permission explicitly
covers the exact edition and language requested. A public webpage, API response,
PDF download, or classical/public-domain original does not automatically grant
rights to a modern translation, edited digital edition, or database.

## Rights to request

Please ask each institution to grant ISLAM 307 explicit written permission for:

1. The exact title, edition, language, translator/editor, ISBN and API resource.
2. Complete-text download and permanent storage in an encrypted or ordinary
   SQLite database.
3. Worldwide offline redistribution in Android, iOS, desktop and web-installable
   application packages.
4. Free and commercial app-store distribution.
5. Local full-text indexing and search.
6. Displaying verse-specific excerpts and full entries.
7. Local AI retrieval and citation **without** model training or generated
   alteration of the Tafseer.
8. Backup, device migration and optional signed content updates.
9. Required attribution wording, logos, links and copyright notices.
10. Permission duration, territories, revocation/expiry terms and whether a fee
    is required.

## Evidence required before import

- Written approval from the rightsholder.
- Exact approved files/API resources and checksums.
- Effective and expiry dates.
- Offline and commercial rights stated explicitly.
- Approved attribution text.
- Any platform, territory, encryption, update or excerpt restrictions.

Until this evidence is received, the corresponding Tafseer must remain
unavailable or a placeholder. It must not be copied into the repository,
preview packs, SQLite database, search index, or AI knowledge source.
